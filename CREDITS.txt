RATCHET & CLANK QUEST VR
An unofficial, non-commercial fan port. It stands entirely on other people's open-source work.

THE MAIN BASE: Rerac-vr by npiriou
This app is a Quest port of Rerac-vr, npiriou's PC VR mod for Ratchet & Clank, and his work is its
main base. From it come the VR design (first person, aiming and shooting with your hand, the weapon
in your hand, quick select by reaching), the Touch controller layout, the comfort choices, the notes
on the hero's items that let this app hide Ratchet and Clank in first person, and every VR feature
still to come. On PC, play Rerac-vr itself. github.com/npiriou/Rerac-vr
ReRAC, the Rust rewrite of Ratchet & Clank that Rerac-vr builds on: the ReRAC contributors.
github.com/re-rac/rerac

THE GAME ENGINE: OpenRAC (openrac.dev)
The decompilation and native port this app runs: the game, its renderer, its Android support,
the mod SDK with the VR view interface, and the tools that read your disc.
Ratchet & Clank PAL (rac1-decomp): Krystof "Lynder063" Malinda, creator and maintainer; Flavius (Veradictus)
Ratchet & Clank NTSC-U (Lombyte): Mateusz Klysz, creator and maintainer; Louis-Philippe Le Sieur, Pezza,
Krystof "Lynder063" Malinda, Purab, mechaivan
The port, launcher tools and level editor: the OpenRAC contributors
OpenRAC credits ReRAC, Wrench, Replanetizer, OpenGOAL's jak-project, splat, spimdisasm, objdiff,
asm-differ, decomp-permuter, m2c, wibo, Himuro, the GNU toolchain, newlib, and the PS2
reverse-engineering and decompilation communities.

MODS BUILT ON: rac1-mods by the OpenRAC contributors
lock-strafe's hooks and strafe logic make Ratchet face where you aim. keyboard-mouse documents the
game's pad report. Difficulty, Item randomizer, Ammo at the most, Xbox button icons and Button swap come
with this app as they are, on the Game tab. github.com/OpenRAC/rac1-mods
