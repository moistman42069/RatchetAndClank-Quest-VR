Options are in two places: these tabs, before you play, and the pause menu, in the game.
Many are only in the game, and that includes all the newest ones.

THE WAY TO THE NEW MOVES (Wall run and jump, Swingshot reach, the wrench's distance)
1. In a level, press Y to pause. Pick Options. Pick VR Options.
2. Hold your hand on the bottom row until VR Controls shows at the bottom. Pick it.
3. Hold your hand on the bottom row until More controls shows at the bottom. Pick it.
4. Pick Moves, the first row.

IN A MENU

POINT         Point your hand at a row, and that row lights up
PICK, CHANGE  Pull the trigger or press A
GO BACK       B
MORE ROWS     A page shows eight rows at a time. The box under the list says where the rest
              are: More below, More up/down or More above.
              Hold your hand on the bottom row and the list moves on, a row at a time. Hold
              it on the top row to move back up. The sticks do not move the list while your
              hand points at the page
LEFT HAND     Pull its trigger to point with it, and again to pick. The right trigger brings
              the right hand back. Menu pointer (More controls) can keep it to one hand

WHERE EVERY OPTION IS IN THE GAME (Y, then Options, then:)

VR OPTIONS    Helmet, Boots, Camera flip, Direction, Player body, Akimbo, Play position, View,
              Cutscenes. Further down, in a level only: Cheats, VR Body, VR Controls

VR HANDS      Gun angle, Gun size, Hand size, Empty hand angle, Items match hand size, Mirror
              left-hand guns, Left gun ammo, HUD size, HUD height, Fingers, Fingers bend around
              the world, and the angle of the weapon you last held

VR OPTIONS, CHEATS
              Invincible, Infinite ammo, Infinite bolts, Big heads, Trippy contrails, Give all
              weapons, Give all gadgets, Unlock all planets

VR OPTIONS, VR BODY
              Player body, Body parts, Tail, Arms bend to your hands, Legs bend, Body turns with
              your head, Body height, Body forward, Arm length, View height, Feet on the floor,
              World scale

VR OPTIONS, VR CONTROLS
              Turning, Snap angle, Smooth turn speed, Physical crouch, Crouch when head drops,
              Vibration, Aim with the right hand, Grab ledges with your hands, Swim with your
              arms, Swing the wrench for real, Throw by hand. Last: More controls

VR OPTIONS, VR CONTROLS, MORE CONTROLS
              Moves, Ledge shimmy, Hide black bars, Menu pointer, Arm moves, Two-handed weapons,
              World collision, World vibration, Walk around your room, Sparkles on the bolts,
              Wheel slow motion, Wheel pick

VR OPTIONS, VR CONTROLS, MORE CONTROLS, MOVES
              Swingshot anywhere, Wall run and jump, Swingshot reach (how far the hook goes),
              Wrench throw distance, Wrench comes back, Hoverboard view (Ahead or Side-on)

The game's own HelpDesk (its tips in voice and text, on for a new game), Save, Load, Sound, Video, Camera and Subtitles
are in the Options list too.
