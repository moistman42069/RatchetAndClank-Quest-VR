IN PLAY, as in Rerac-vr on PC

LEFT STICK    Walk, towards where you look (or your body's facing: Direction in VR Options)
RIGHT STICK   Turn (smooth by default; snap in the VR tab). In third person, the game's camera
A             Jump. Twice for a double jump
B             Hover and strafe with Clank's packs, Hydro-Pack boost
X             Map. In a cutscene: how it is seen, through his eyes, in VR or on a screen
              (only the cutscene's view: you play on in your View when it ends)
Y             Pause. Skips a cutscene
RIGHT TRIGGER Fire where your hand points. With an empty hand, takes your last gun or glove back out
              Gloves: hold the trigger, throw with your hand and let go. Let go slowly to keep it
              Walloper: punch for real
RIGHT GRIP    Takes the wrench out. Swing your hand for real to hit, weak taps do not count
              Let go mid-swing to throw it, it comes back. Let go slowly to put it away: your hand
              is empty, which is how to put a gun or a glove down
              In the air, slam it down for the Hyper Strike
              Near a bolt: hold it on the bolt and walk around it
LEFT GRIP or TRIGGER   Crouch, and the packs (hold with A for long and high jumps, flips)
              Or crouch for real: lower your head (Physical crouch, VR Controls). With it on,
              the left grip crouches no more and the hand is free; the trigger still does it all
              (with Akimbo's gun in the left hand, the grip stays the crouch)
              Near the front of a big weapon, the left grip holds it with both hands
RIGHT STICK CLICK      Talk, and quick select: hold, move your right hand toward a weapon, let go
                       Pull the right trigger while holding it for the next page
LEFT STICK CLICK       Akimbo (VR Options, on by default): the same for your left hand, on a ring of
                       its own. Both can be open at once. Left trigger fires it, or turns its
                       page while the ring is open; what it cannot hold is greyed. Pick what it
                       holds to empty it. A glove is thrown by the left hand, and the Walloper
                       (or a left punch) and the Swingshot take over as your main hand while
                       they are used. The Drone Device picked there launches its drones.
                       On the hoverboard the left weapon is put away
LEFT MENU BUTTON       Recentre (holding the headset's own button does it too)

LEDGES        Squeeze a grip near the edge as you fall. To shimmy, hold your hands out the way you
              want to go (right to go right), or push the left stick. Ledge shimmy (More controls):
              Pull slides your hands the other way instead. One hand holds, and the other can shoot;
              the holding hand's gun is put away until it lets go. To climb, pull yourself up: draw
              a holding hand (or both) down toward your chest. Pushing both down fast, or A, climbs
              too. Let go of both to drop
SWIMMING      Breaststroke with your hands, palms pushing back. Push the water up to dive
HYDRODISPLACER, TRESPASSER   Push the tool into its slot, or pull the trigger on it
TRESPASSER RINGS   The sticks turn them. Y, the click or the trigger takes the tool out. They hang
              over the tool you plugged in (a setting puts them before your face)
VISIBOMB      Fire it, then the left stick flies the missile, A speeds it up and the trigger sets
              it off. Its picture is on the gun's own screen, in your hand
SWINGSHOT     At a yellow or green target, hold the trigger. With Swingshot anywhere on (VR Controls,
              More controls, off to start): in the right hand it zips you to any wall or ground in
              reach, in the left (Akimbo) it swings you from a point above your head. Hold to zip or
              swing, let go to drop off with your speed; lean to push a swing. One in each hand to
              chain them
ARM MOVES     Arm moves on Buttons (More controls, For real to start), for hands and wrists that
              should not swing: the right grip swings the wrench (again for the next blow; in the
              air, the jump attack), the trigger punches with the Walloper, and as Giant Clank the
              grip punches. The trigger throws a glove's bomb, ledges are caught for you, the left
              stick swims. Your hands still aim

ON THE HOVERBOARD

Left stick steers, A jumps, left trigger boosts, right trigger shoots.
In the air: right stick for tricks (or the left grip, X, B), left stick to spin.

AS CLANK AND GIANT CLANK

Punch for real, with either hand. The right stick click gives the little robots their orders,
and is Giant Clank's beam. The right trigger is Giant Clank's missiles. Both bodies face where
you look, so the missiles and the beam go there.

TURRETS AND SHIPS

Aim a turret with your right hand, the trigger fires, the click leaves. Flying, the trigger is the
lasers, X and the left trigger the missiles.

IN MENUS

Point with your right hand and pull the trigger or press A. On a page you can point at, the sticks
do nothing (if the right controller is off, they move the cursor). The map pans with its sticks;
there A shows the planet's missions and the trigger plays its infobot.
B goes back, Y closes the pause menu. VR Options: View switches first and third person at any time,
Cutscenes how the scenes are seen.
The left grip and trigger turn the pages.
Menu pointer (More controls): with Both hands, pull the left trigger at the menu to point with the
left hand (that pull only moves the pointer), the right trigger to go back to the right. While the
left points, its trigger picks and the right grip and trigger turn the pages. Right hand or Left
hand keeps the pointer on one.
Hide black bars (More controls, on): no black bars at the top and bottom in the game's short
scenes during play, such as the start of Oltanis's grind race, in first or third person.
Sound page: hold the trigger on a bar and move along it to set the volume.
A box with key labels (Yes, No, OK): point at a label and pull the trigger or press A.

AT A VENDOR

You stay on your feet: left stick and your room walk, A jumps. Walk away and it closes.
The right stick browses, the right trigger works on what you point at (Buy, Exit, an item, the
popup's lines and arrows), the right stick click leaves.
With "Shop on foot" off, the game's own screen: A buys.
The PDA's vendor stands before you the same way, and stays until you leave it.

IN 2D MODE

The controllers are a PlayStation pad: A Cross, B Circle, X Square, Y Triangle,
triggers L1 and R1, grips L2 and R2, sticks as sticks, the left menu button Start,
the left stick click Select.
