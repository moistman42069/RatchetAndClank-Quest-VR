IN PLAY IN FIRST PERSON, as in Rerac-vr on PC (third person too with Third person controls on VR)

LEFT STICK    Walk, towards where you look (or your body's facing: Direction in VR Options)
RIGHT STICK   Turn (smooth by default; snap in the VR tab). In third person, the game's camera
A             Jump. Twice for a double jump
B             Hover and strafe with Clank's packs, Hydro-Pack boost
X             Map. In a cutscene: how it is seen, through his eyes or in VR (only the cutscene's
              view: you play on in your View when it ends). VR Options' Cutscenes puts it on a screen
Y             Pause. Skips a cutscene
RIGHT TRIGGER Fire where your hand points. With an empty hand, takes your last gun or glove back out
              Gloves: hold the trigger, throw with your hand and let go. Let go slowly to keep it
              Walloper: punch for real
RIGHT GRIP    Takes the wrench out. Swing your hand for real to hit, weak taps do not count
              It holds until you open the grip well past halfway: a loose grip in a swing keeps it
              Open your hand mid-swing to throw it, it comes back. Let go slowly to put it away:
              your hand is empty, which is how to put a gun or a glove down
              In the air, slam it down for the Hyper Strike
              At a bolt crank: hold the wrench's head on the bolt, then hold the trigger and it
              turns by itself, or turn it round with your hand (Turning bolts, Moves: Walk around
              walks you round it with the stick instead). It works in first person
LEFT TRIGGER  Crouch, and the packs (hold with A for long and high jumps, flips)
              Or crouch for real: lower your head (Physical crouch, VR Controls)
              At the water's surface, in first or third person, it dives
LEFT GRIP     On foot in first person it only closes your left hand (a fist, a ledge): the
              trigger crouches, Physical crouch on or off. With Akimbo's gun in the left hand,
              or in Third person, the grip crouches too
              Near the front of a big weapon, the left grip holds it with both hands
RIGHT STICK CLICK      Talk, and the weapon wheel: hold, point your right hand at a weapon, let go.
                       The wheel opens where your hand is (each hand's at that hand).
                       Time slows while you hold it (More controls, Weapon wheel: its slow motion,
                       how slow, Wheel pick, Swap at full speed, and the left hand's own slots).
                       In a party only you slow, in the air, until every player has it up.
                       Pull the right trigger while holding it for the next page. The pointer
                       stays inside the wheel, and each weapon you pass clicks in your hand.
                       A Swingshot you hang from, or that pulls you in, holds while it is open.
                       Rest the dot in its middle a moment and let go: the hand empty (the
                       trigger brings the gun back; Wheel pick on Point, first person)
LEFT STICK CLICK       Akimbo (VR Options, on by default): the same for your left hand, on a ring of
                       its own. Both can be open at once. Left trigger fires it, or turns its
                       page while the ring is open; what it cannot hold is greyed. Pick what it
                       holds, or let go in its middle, to empty it. A glove is thrown by the left hand, and the Walloper
                       (or a left punch) and the Swingshot take over as your main hand while
                       they are used. The Drone Device picked there launches its drones.
                       On the hoverboard the left weapon is put away
LEFT MENU BUTTON       Recentre (holding the headset's own button does it too)
                       Hold it a second in play: switch first and third person
                       (in third person with Gamepad a tap is Start, as you let go)

LEDGES        Squeeze a grip near the edge as you fall. To shimmy, hold your hands out the way you
              want to go (right to go right), or push the left stick. Ledge shimmy (More controls):
              Pull slides your hands the other way instead. One hand holds, and the other can shoot;
              the holding hand's gun is put away until it lets go. To climb, pull yourself up: draw
              a holding hand (or both) down toward your chest. Pushing both down fast, or A, climbs
              too. Let go of both to drop
SWIMMING      Breaststroke with your hands, palms pushing back, or the left stick. To dive: the left
              trigger, push both hands up quickly (any way you hold them), or look well down and
              stroke. Under the water you go where you look: look down and push the stick (or
              stroke) to go deeper, look up to come back up
HYDRODISPLACER, TRESPASSER   Push the tool into its slot, or pull the trigger on it
TRESPASSER RINGS   Each push of a stick turns the chosen ring one notch (held, another after a
              moment, then a few a second); up and down choose the ring. A turns it one notch
              clockwise, B anticlockwise, X picks the next ring. Or squeeze a grip with your
              hand over a ring to hold it, and move your hand round the middle: a notch for
              each twelfth of a circle ("Grab the Trespasser's rings", on to start). Y, the
              click or the trigger takes the tool out. They hang over the tool you plugged in
              (a setting puts them before your face, out of reach of the grab)
VISIBOMB      Fire it, then the left stick flies the missile, A speeds it up and the trigger sets
              it off. Its picture is on the gun's own screen, in your hand
SWINGSHOT     At a yellow or green target, hold the trigger. With Swingshot anywhere on (VR Controls,
              More controls, Moves; off to start): in the right hand it zips you to any wall or ground
              in reach, in the left (Akimbo) it swings you from a point above your head. Hold to zip or
              swing, let go to drop off with your speed; lean to push a swing. One in each hand to
              chain them. Yank the hand that fired, hard, away from where you want to go: a
              fling, on along the swing or off the zip. Swingshot reach (Moves): how far the hook goes
GRAB ENEMIES  With Swingshot grabs enemies on (Moves, off to start): aim the Swingshot at an enemy
              where there is no target and pull the trigger. The hook bites into it and yanks it in
              to hang before your hand. Swing your hand and let go of the trigger to throw it (half
              a wrench blow where it lands, and to an enemy it hits), or let go gently to drop it.
              Up to six seconds. Big enemies can't be caught. Crates too, with Grab crates (Grab
              moves, on to start): a thrown crate breaks where it lands and hits what it meets
GRAB MOVES    Each off to start (Moves, Grab moves): flick your hand while it comes in to fling it
              from there; let go gently to stun it; let go swinging up to throw it up high, and hook
              it again up there to hang it on the line; let go swinging down, or yank down while it
              hangs, to slam it; jump while it hangs on the line to zip to it. Pits and lava finish
              them
WALLS         With Wall run and jump on (Moves, off to start; first person), in the air by a wall: the
              stick along the wall runs along it, the stick into it runs up it, the stick away drops
              you off. A zip that ends at a wall holds you there. The jump button leaps you off, where
              your head looks, whatever the stick is doing; look up to go higher. Each wall gives one
              run and one leap until you land or fire the Swingshot
WRENCH THROW  Wrench throw distance (Moves): how far it goes. Wrench comes back (Moves): When you
              call it, it stays where it lands; a fresh squeeze of the right grip or the trigger calls
              it back, and pressing for the ring or grabbing a ledge does too. Wrench aim (Moves):
              Wrench throw aim: Where you throw (to start), the way your hand went at its fastest as
              you let go; Along your swing; or Where you point, the controller as your hand opens.
              Wrench aim help: how hard the wrench is pulled onto an enemy or crate as you let go,
              Off, Light (0.1.23's few degrees), Medium, Strong (to start) or Very strong; from
              Medium up it also finds what you look at, so a swing from the side or across lands,
              and it steers on to it in flight. Wrench throw angle: from 30 down to 30 up, Level to
              start
PUNCHES       With Punch with your hands on (Moves, off to start): a quick jab out from your body with
              a free hand, or the hand holding a gun, hits what it meets for half a wrench blow.
              Open hand or fist, no button. The wrench, gloves and Walloper do their own; the
              Swingshot's jab hits too, but not while its hook is out
ARM MOVES    Arm moves on Buttons (More controls, For real to start), for hands and wrists that
              should not swing: the right grip swings the wrench (again for the next blow; in the
              air, the jump attack), the trigger punches with the Walloper, and as Giant Clank the
              grip punches. The trigger throws a glove's bomb, ledges are caught for you, the left
              stick swims. Your hands still aim

ON THE HOVERBOARD

Left stick steers, A jumps, left trigger boosts, right trigger shoots.
In the air: right stick for tricks (or the left grip, X, B), left stick to spin.
The view looks where the board goes. Hoverboard view (Moves): Side-on looks along his chest.

ON RAC-BOARD (a mod: the launcher's Game tab, off to start)

Reach behind your back with either hand (it buzzes there) and click that hand's stick to get on
the board, and again to get off. In first person the left stick steers to where you look, as on
foot (ahead brings the board round to your gaze, the short way; in third person, about its
heading), A jumps.
He keeps his weapon as he rides (RAC-Board's Weapons on the board, on to start): the right
trigger fires it and the wheel opens as on foot, the right grip takes the wrench (swing your
hand), B does tricks (with the left stick back, a flip; with A by a rail, a grind), the left grip
(or the left trigger with no gun in that hand) grabs the board. The Swingshot keeps him on the
board: he rides on as a swing ends, a pull sets him down on it held still until you push the
stick, and Swingshot grabs catch and throw enemies as he rides.
With Weapons on the board off, he takes the wrench as he gets on, the right trigger does the
tricks and the right grip grinds, as before. Your view goes with its flips and spins, not with
each carve or the ride's own sway. In third person with Gamepad the mod's settings are your
controllers as an Xbox controller's (to start: B fires, Y opens the quick select, X swings the wrench, RB or RT tricks,
with A by a rail grinds, LB grabs, D-pad Up gets on and off; the reach still works), or as
your Third person buttons put them: the board's R1, R2 and L1 are wherever you put those.

AS CLANK AND GIANT CLANK

Punch for real, with either hand. The right stick click gives the little robots their orders,
and is Giant Clank's beam. The right trigger is Giant Clank's missiles. Both bodies face where
you look, so the missiles and the beam go there.

TURRETS AND SHIPS

Aim a turret with your right hand, the trigger fires, the click leaves. Flying, the trigger is the
lasers, X and the left trigger the missiles.

IN MENUS

Point with your right hand and pull the trigger or press A. On a page you can point at, the sticks
do nothing (if the right controller is off, they move the cursor). The map pans with its sticks;
there A shows the planet's missions and the trigger plays its infobot.
B goes back, Y closes the pause menu. VR Options: View switches first and third person at any time,
Cutscenes how the scenes are seen.
The left grip and trigger turn the pages.
Menu pointer (More controls): with Both hands, pull the left trigger at the menu to point with the
left hand (that pull only moves the pointer), the right trigger to go back to the right. While the
left points, its trigger picks and the right grip and trigger turn the pages. Right hand or Left
hand keeps the pointer on one.
Hide black bars (More controls, on): no black bars at the top and bottom in the game's short
scenes during play, such as the start of Oltanis's grind race, in first or third person.
Sound page: hold the trigger on a bar and move along it to set the volume.
A box with key labels (Yes, No, OK): point at a label and pull the trigger or press A.

AT A VENDOR

You stay on your feet: left stick and your room walk, A jumps. Walk away and it closes.
The right stick browses, the right trigger works on what you point at (Buy, Exit, an item, the
popup's lines and arrows), the right stick click leaves.
With "Shop on foot" off, the game's own screen: A buys.
The PDA's vendor stands before you the same way, and stays until you leave it.

IN THIRD PERSON WITH GAMEPAD (the VR tab's Third person controls, to start), AND IN 2D MODE

The controllers are a gamepad. To start (from 0.1.24), as in first person: the right trigger
fires (Circle), the right grip swings the wrench (Square), A jumps (Cross), the right stick
click is quick select and talk (Triangle), the left trigger and B crouch (R1 and R2), the left
grip and Y look around (L1 and L2), X and the left stick click open the map (Select and R3),
sticks as sticks, the left menu button Start (in third person as you let it go; held a second
in play, back to first person). In third person's menus the Xbox layout and the pointer: A and
the trigger pick what you point at. Recentre with the headset's own (hold Meta).
THIRD PERSON BUTTONS (the VR tab, under D-pad): choose the button for each action yourself.
Tap an action, then a button; if another action had that button, the two swap, so no button
ever does two things. "Like first person" is the layout above. "Xbox controller" lays them out
as a real gamepad: A jump (Cross), B fire (Circle), X wrench (Square), Y quick select and talk
(Triangle), the grips LB and RB (L1 and R1: RB crouches, LB looks around), the triggers LT and
RT (L2 and R2: RT crouches too, LT looks), the left stick click Select (the map), the right
stick click R3 (the map too); 0.1.19 to 0.1.23 started there. A layout you chose stays. The
menu button always pauses, and the menus keep the Xbox layout. The game's hints show the
buttons you chose.
D-PAD (the VR tab): Controller by your head, to start: hold a controller up beside your head,
as a phone at your ear; it buzzes, and its stick is the D-pad (its click L3 or R3) while it
stays there. Left thumb rest: the right stick, while your left thumb rests on its rest.
To 0.1.18 2D mode had the triggers on L1 and R1, the grips on L2 and R2.

THE BUTTONS IN THE GAME'S HINTS

Xbox-style, in first and third person: each shows the button that does it in the view you
play (in first person Circle's hints show RT, the trigger that fires). In colour with the
Xbox button icons mod on (the Game tab, on to start).
