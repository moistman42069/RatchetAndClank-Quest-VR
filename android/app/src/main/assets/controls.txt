LEFT STICK    Walk, towards where you look
RIGHT STICK   Turn (smooth by default; snap in the VR tab). In third person, the game's camera
A             Jump. Twice for a double jump
B             Crouch, or Clank's packs in the air
X             Map
Y             Pause. Skips a cutscene
RIGHT TRIGGER Fire
RIGHT GRIP    Swing the wrench
LEFT TRIGGER or GRIP   Crouch
RIGHT STICK CLICK      Talk, and quick select: hold, move your right hand toward a weapon, let go
LEFT STICK CLICK       Look through Ratchet's eyes (the game's first-person view)
BOTH STICKS CLICKED    Recentre

In menus: either stick moves, A or the trigger takes, B goes back.

Long jump, high jump, flips: as in the game, Crouch + A while moving or standing.
Layout after Rerac-vr, the PC VR mod.
