FIXED THAT DARN THUMB!

    Sorry about that one. 0.1.14 turned your thumb the wrong way, back toward your wrist, and it got out because
    we did not test it thoroughly enough before the release. Our apologies for the hiccup.

    It now moves like a thumb. Lifted off the controller, it rests open beside your hand. On a button, the stick
    or the thumb rest, it comes in along your fingers. Close your hand and a lifted thumb is a thumbs-up, and a
    thumb on the controller folds over your fingers. Both hands, on Quest and on PC.

    We looked at it on a PC from all around each hand, with a simulated headset. Tell us how it looks in yours.

CAN'T FIND THE NEW OPTIONS? LONG MENUS NOW TELL YOU THERE IS MORE.

    They are in the game, not in this launcher. In a level, press Y, then Options, VR Options, VR Controls, More
    controls, Moves. A page shows eight rows at a time, and the box under the list now says where the rest are:
    More below, More up/down or More above. Hold your hand on the bottom row and the list moves on, on the top
    row and it moves back. The Controls tab has a Menus section with where every option is.

What 0.1.14 brought, in case you missed it:

1.  Now on PC VR too. The release has a Windows download beside this APK: the same launcher, options and game,
    as RatchetClankVR.exe, for SteamVR, Meta Horizon Link, Virtual Desktop, Pico Connect, Windows Mixed
    Reality and other PC VR software. Touch, Index, Vive, Pico, WMR and Cosmos controllers are known by name;
    others (PSVR2, Steam Frame, DPVR) come through SteamVR.

2.  Wall run and jump (new, in play: VR Options, VR Controls, More controls, Moves; Off to start). In the air by a
    wall, the stick along it runs Ratchet along the wall, and the stick into it runs him up it. A zip that ends
    at a wall holds him there instead of dropping him. The jump button leaps him off where your head looks,
    even with the stick still held along the wall, and his speed goes with him, so zips, swings and walls chain.

3.  Swingshot reach (new, Moves): Normal is the game's, Longer is twice that, No limit reaches any surface you can
    aim at, right across a level. A far zip goes faster than a near one. A left hand's shot too far to swing
    from zips instead. With Swingshot anywhere off, it is how far the game's own targets can be hooked from.

4.  The wrench thrown far (new, Moves). Wrench throw distance: Far, Very far and Super far go 2, 4 and 8 times as
    far, in an arc. Wrench comes back: When you call it, it stays where it lands until you squeeze the right
    grip or pull the trigger, and it comes whirling back with your hand buzzing harder as it nears. Every
    catch now has a thump in the hand, whichever you choose.

5.  Swingshot anywhere has moved to Moves, with a tester's notes in it. Fling: yank the hand that fired, hard, away
    from where you want to go, on a swing to throw it on, on a zip to leap off it. Zips chain in the air, with
    no need to touch the ground between them. The hook no longer cancels the Heli-Pack's glide: he stays up
    while it flies, and with jump held the glide comes back. A press aimed at nothing keeps trying while you
    hold it.

6.  The Swingshot in your hand. With one in each hand, each has its own hook: the hook no longer lags behind or
    hangs in the air, and firing one no longer fires the other. It now lies along your aim, however you hold the
    controller. If you set a Swingshot angle before (VR Hands), put it back to Default.

7.  Keep the refresh rate (new, Graphics, On to start). The headset stays at the rate you chose, such as 120 Hz,
    so wherever the game keeps up it is that smooth. Before, where the game could not hold the rate for twenty
    seconds, the headset dropped back to 72 Hz for the rest of the run. Off brings that back.

8.  The left grip only closes your hand, on foot in first person, Physical crouch on or off. The left trigger
    crouches and works the packs. With Akimbo's gun in the left hand, or in third person, the grip still
    crouches.

9.  Empty hand angle starts at 65 down. If yours was at the old start, 0, it moves to 65 down once; an angle
    you set yourself stays.

10. Walking backwards, Ratchet no longer slides forward when you let go of the stick. Walking aside, he no
    longer slides ahead either.

11. No warped picture at the start: the copyright lines after the intro movie, and the save select's picture
    as a level's first scene begins, are no longer held in front of you as you move your head.
