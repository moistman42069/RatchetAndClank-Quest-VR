1.  Swingshot anywhere (new, Off to start). Switch it on in the game: pause, VR Options, VR Controls, More
    controls, first row. Where there is no Swingshot target in reach, the whole level is one. In your right
    hand it zips you to whatever wall or ground you aim it at; in your left hand (Akimbo) it swings you from any
    point above your head. Hold the trigger to zip or swing, let go to drop off: a zip let go midway keeps your
    speed, as a swing does. Lean in your room to push a swing. One in each hand: swing, let go, zip, and on. The
    game's own targets work as before, and with it off the Swingshot is the game's. Not on Novalis, whose game
    has no Swingshot moves.

2.  Arm moves (new, More controls, For real to start). On Buttons, nothing asks you to swing: squeeze the right
    grip to swing the wrench (again for the next blow, and in the air for the jump attack), pull the trigger to
    punch with the Walloper, and as Giant Clank the grip punches. The trigger throws a glove's bomb, ledges are
    caught for you and the left stick swims. Your hands still aim. For hands and wrists that hurt to swing.

3.  Shimmy along a ledge the way you reach. Hanging on, hold your hands out to the right to go right, to the
    left to go left. The left stick shimmies too. Ledge shimmy (More controls) brings back the old way, Pull.
    The hand holding the ledge puts its gun away until it lets go: no gun floating over your hands.

4.  Hide black bars (More controls, On). No black bars at the top and bottom in the game's short scenes
    during play, such as the start of Oltanis's grind race, in first or third person. Off gives the game's
    own back.

5.  Point with either hand in menus. Menu pointer (More controls): Both hands, the start, points with the
    right hand until you pull the left trigger at the menu, and then with the left until you pull the right
    one; that pull only moves the pointer. Right hand or Left hand keeps it on one. While the left points, its
    trigger picks and the right grip and trigger turn the pages.

6.  The map's two buttons. A shows the planet's missions and the trigger plays its infobot, as their icons
    say. Before, both played the infobot.

7.  The ammo box follows your gun again after switching between two weapons, such as the Pyrocitor and the
    Bomb Glove, where it could stay on the left hand's gun.

8.  Climbing out of your ship, the view no longer dips through the hull: it stays at the seat's height until
    Ratchet jumps out.

9.  Akimbo is on for everyone. Click the left stick and pick a gun to hold one in each hand; pick it again to
    empty the hand. Until you do, your left hand, trigger and grip work as before.

10. Climb ledges by pulling yourself up. Hanging on, draw a holding hand down toward your chest, one hand or
    both. The quick push down and A still climb.

11. Checked: grinding a rail, either hand's gun fires; on a cable, the left hand's (Akimbo), the right holding on.

12. An angle for each weapon, the Swingshot too. VR Hands' last row is named after the item you last drew,
    such as "Swingshot angle": it turns that item alone in your hand, on top of Gun angle. For the Swingshot it
    turns the housing on your wrist, so you can line it up with where the hook flies.

13. The whole body to start. Body parts (VR Body) now starts on Everything. A choice you made yourself stays.

14. Fingers and head in the shadow. With "Shadow matches what you see" on, each finger and your head cast their
    own shadow.

15. The left grip only closes your hand with Physical crouch on. Lower your head to crouch, or use the left
    trigger, which still does all the grip did.

16. Empty hand angle (VR Hands) now goes to 65 down.

17. VR Controls' last rows can be reached: the page now ends with More controls, which holds them. Before, the
    last four could not be reached.

18. "Hide Ratchet's body" is gone from the VR tab. Switched off, it put your view inside his head.
