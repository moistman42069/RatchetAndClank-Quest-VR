NEW IN 0.1.22

THE REFRESH RATE STEPS DOWN, NOT STRAIGHT TO 72

    With Keep the refresh rate off (Graphics), a rate the game could not hold for twenty seconds sent the headset
    straight back to 72 Hz. With Smoother frames on that meant 36 pictures a second for the game's 60 steps, the
    stutter that running Smoother frames at 120 Hz was made to end. Now it steps down one rate at a time: to 90 Hz
    (45 pictures a second with Smoother frames), and only if 90 is not held either, to 72. Keep the refresh rate on
    (the start) is unchanged: the headset stays where you put it.

THE PDA, ONCE MORE

    More reports of the PDA crashing the game came in, all on 0.1.19: the same crash 0.1.21 fixed. We opened the
    PDA's vendor from the hand three times in a row, with a walk between, and the game kept going each time.

ALSO NEW: ALL OF 0.1.21, WHICH CAME OUT JUST BEFORE, IN CASE YOU MISSED IT

THIRD PERSON BUTTONS: EACH ACTION WHERE YOU WANT IT

    A tester on 0.1.19: in third person "the swinging with the hammer is then assigned to the X button on the left
    controller, which makes no sense because one uses the left controller/stick for movement." Third person with
    Gamepad, and Play in 2D, had one layout, an Xbox controller's. Now Third person buttons (VR tab, under D-pad)
    lets you choose: tap an action (jump, fire, the wrench, quick select, crouch, look, the map), then the button
    for it. If another action had that button the two swap, so no button ever does two things and nothing is lost.
    Like first person puts them as in first person: the right trigger fires, the right grip swings the wrench, A
    jumps, the right stick click is quick select, the left trigger and B crouch, the left grip and Y look, X and the
    left stick click the map. Xbox controller (the start) is the layout from before. The menu button always pauses
    (held, it switches the view), the menus keep the Xbox layout, and the game's hints show the buttons you chose.
    RAC-Board's tricks, grinds and grabs follow the buttons you give R1, R2 and L1.

THE TRESPASSER'S RINGS: ONE NOTCH A PUSH, OR TURN THEM BY HAND

    A tester: "my joystick try to fight me". The sticks worked as a D-pad held down, about twelve notches a second,
    and a push a little off straight changed the ring as well. Now each push of a stick turns the chosen ring one
    notch (held, another after a moment), up and down choose the ring, A and B turn it a notch each way, X picks the
    next ring and Y leaves. New, Grab the Trespasser's rings (VR tab, on to start): with your hand over a ring,
    squeeze the grip to hold it and move your hand round the middle to turn it, a notch for each twelfth of a
    circle. It needs The Trespasser's rings over the tool. Each notch is still the game's own turn, with its sound.
    Play in 2D keeps the game's own controls.

A CRASH AT THE PDA'S VENDOR

    A tester: "pocket gadgetron thing you buy from quark crashed game". With the PDA in your hand the trigger opens
    its vendor. The game keeps that press for a few frames, and while you shop on foot Ratchet's own code runs, so
    it opened the vendor a second time over the first; that emptied the vendor's screens and the game stopped. Now
    the vendor that is open stays as it is. We made the crash happen on a PC as the tester had it, the PDA in the
    right hand and a gun in the left, and now it no longer does, with the gun or without.

SCENES: CHECKED

    A tester saw scenes on a screen that looked as if they could play around you. We checked them all: every scene
    the game draws itself plays in VR (Cutscenes, VR tab). The ones on a screen are the game's movies, videos on the
    disc with no 3D scene behind them, so they can't be shown around you. The ending's slideshow is still pictures,
    shown flat too.

AND ALL OF 0.1.20, THE RELEASE BEFORE THAT

THE GAME STARTS IN THE VIEW THE LAUNCHER SAYS

    A tester's game kept starting in third person with the launcher set to first person. Holding the left menu
    button for a second in play switches the view, and until now the game kept that switch for its next start:
    hold it once by chance, while recentring say, and every start after that was third person, where the
    controllers are now a gamepad. Now the game always starts in the launcher's View (VR tab). The hold still
    switches until you close the game, and the switch now says which view you are in and how to get back, such
    as THIRD PERSON  HOLD LEFT MENU FOR FIRST PERSON. If you like third person with the VR controls, set Third
    person controls to VR (VR tab).

SMOOTHER FRAMES: THE GAME DRAWS 60 PICTURES A SECOND

    A tester found the micro stutters gone with the headset at 120 Hz. With Smoother frames on (the start),
    the game draws every other frame, so at 72 Hz, where every preset put the Refresh rate, it drew only 36
    pictures a second for its own 60 steps a second. Now Smoother frames runs the headset at 120 Hz and the
    game draws 60, one for each step; the Refresh rate is for Smoother frames off. If the game cannot keep up
    (a stutter, or OVR Metrics under 60), lower Eye resolution. With Keep the refresh rate off, the menus no
    longer drop the headset to 72 Hz as play begins.

NOTICES FADE AWAY

    The words shown ahead of you, such as a controller not found or the view switched, now fade away after a
    couple of seconds instead of staying.

MAGNEBOOTS IN FIRST PERSON

    On a magnet wall your eyes were flat against it ("like if you was licking the magnet wall", a tester), and
    upside down they were on the other side of the surface. Your eyes now sit at Ratchet's eye height off
    whatever he walks on, walls and ceilings alike, as on the ground.

A CRASH AFTER DROWNING, WITH SOMETHING IN THE LEFT HAND

    A tester's game crashed as Ratchet drowned at Qwark's base on Umbris while the left hand held the Swingshot
    (Akimbo). As he comes back after a death the game makes its things again, and the left hand gave the right
    hand back an item that by then was something else, so the game read where nothing was. Now, if the game
    remade things while the left hand held an item, the left hand lets it go and the right hand keeps what the
    game gave it.

GLOWS IN THE TAKE-OFF AND THE FLIGHT

    Seen through Ratchet's eyes (Cutscenes: Through his eyes), a take-off's glows and sparks turned to face the pad
    you left, not your eyes, and in the flight after it they turned with the ship. They now face your eyes in
    the scene, and the game's camera in the flight, as the flight is seen from it.

STILL LOOKING INTO

    The cockpit window racing the ship between planets, and Blarg station's far windows acting strangely: we
    could not make either happen here yet. If you see one, a short clip, or where you were (the planet, the
    room, which way you faced), helps a lot.

0.1.15: FIXED THAT DARN THUMB!

    Sorry about that one. 0.1.14 turned your thumb the wrong way, back toward your wrist, and it got out because
    we did not test it thoroughly enough before the release. Our apologies for the hiccup.

    It now moves like a thumb. Lifted off the controller, it rests open beside your hand. On a button, the stick
    or the thumb rest, it comes in along your fingers. Close your hand and a lifted thumb is a thumbs-up, and a
    thumb on the controller folds over your fingers. Both hands, on Quest and on PC.

    We looked at it on a PC from all around each hand, with a simulated headset. Tell us how it looks in yours.

What 0.1.14 brought, in case you missed it:

1.  Now on PC VR too. The release has a Windows download beside this APK: the same launcher, options and game,
    as RatchetClankVR.exe, for SteamVR, Meta Horizon Link, Virtual Desktop, Pico Connect, Windows Mixed
    Reality and other PC VR software. Touch, Index, Vive, Pico, WMR and Cosmos controllers are known by name;
    others (PSVR2, Steam Frame, DPVR) come through SteamVR.

2.  Wall run and jump (new, in play: VR Options, VR Controls, More controls, Moves; Off to start). In the air by a
    wall, the stick along it runs Ratchet along the wall, and the stick into it runs him up it. A zip that ends
    at a wall holds him there instead of dropping him. The jump button leaps him off where your head looks,
    even with the stick still held along the wall, and his speed goes with him, so zips, swings and walls chain.

3.  Swingshot reach (new, Moves): Normal is the game's, Longer is twice that, No limit reaches any surface you can
    aim at, right across a level. A far zip goes faster than a near one. A left hand's shot too far to swing
    from zips instead. With Swingshot anywhere off, it is how far the game's own targets can be hooked from.

4.  The wrench thrown far (new, Moves). Wrench throw distance: Far, Very far and Super far go 2, 4 and 8 times as
    far, in an arc. Wrench comes back: When you call it, it stays where it lands until you squeeze the right
    grip or pull the trigger, and it comes whirling back with your hand buzzing harder as it nears. Every
    catch now has a thump in the hand, whichever you choose.

5.  Swingshot anywhere has moved to Moves, with a tester's notes in it. Fling: yank the hand that fired, hard, away
    from where you want to go, on a swing to throw it on, on a zip to leap off it. Zips chain in the air, with
    no need to touch the ground between them. The hook no longer cancels the Heli-Pack's glide: he stays up
    while it flies, and with jump held the glide comes back. A press aimed at nothing keeps trying while you
    hold it.

6.  The Swingshot in your hand. With one in each hand, each has its own hook: the hook no longer lags behind or
    hangs in the air, and firing one no longer fires the other. It now lies along your aim, however you hold the
    controller. If you set a Swingshot angle before (VR Hands), put it back to Default.

7.  Keep the refresh rate (new, Graphics, On to start). The headset stays at the rate you chose, such as 120 Hz,
    so wherever the game keeps up it is that smooth. Before, where the game could not hold the rate for twenty
    seconds, the headset dropped back to 72 Hz for the rest of the run. Off brings that back.

8.  The left grip only closes your hand, on foot in first person, Physical crouch on or off. The left trigger
    crouches and works the packs. With Akimbo's gun in the left hand, or in third person, the grip still
    crouches.

9.  Empty hand angle starts at 65 down. If yours was at the old start, 0, it moves to 65 down once; an angle
    you set yourself stays.

10. Walking backwards, Ratchet no longer slides forward when you let go of the stick. Walking aside, he no
    longer slides ahead either.

11. No warped picture at the start: the copyright lines after the intro movie, and the save select's picture
    as a level's first scene begins, are no longer held in front of you as you move your head.
