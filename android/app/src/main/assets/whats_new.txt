NEW IN 0.1.24

THE WRENCH FINDS ITS TARGET

    Wrench aim help now works as you let go, from any angle: swing from the side or across your body and the
    wrench goes to the enemy or crate you look at, and steers on to it as it flies. Wrench aim (a new page in
    Moves) sets how hard: Off, Light (0.1.23's), Medium, Strong (to start) or Very strong.

THROW CRATES

    With Swingshot grabs enemies on, the Swingshot catches crates too (Grab crates, Grab moves, on to start):
    throw one at an enemy for half a wrench blow, and it breaks where it lands.

THE HOOK BITES

    The Swingshot's hook hung where it was fired while the chain went to the enemy. It now flies out fast, bites
    into the enemy's side, claws along the chain, and stays on it as it moves, is held, and is thrown.

BACK TO 0 BOLTS

    Cheats has Set bolts to 0, which turns Infinite bolts off too: explore for bolts again after a full wallet.

THIRD PERSON: LIKE FIRST PERSON TO START

    Third person buttons start Like first person: the right trigger fires, the right grip swings the wrench. If
    you never changed them, that is what you get now; a layout you chose stays. Xbox controller is still there.

MULTIPLAYER: WATER AND MORE FOR EVERYONE

    A player who joined a party saw no water: no seas, no lava, no fire or smoke, while the host's game drew all
    of it. The game draws those through the things that own them, and a joined game had left those to the host's.
    Now every game draws its own, and water the host's switches raise or lower (Rilgar, Hoven, Pokitaru, Umbris)
    follows the host's height in yours. It needs 0.1.24 in the game that joins; the host can be any version, or a
    flat PC. With a host on 0.1.24 too, on the same disc (PAL with PAL, US with US), what the host's enemies shoot
    and the blasts they make show in your game, and Hoven's reservoir drains in yours when the host's drains.

THE LEFT TESLA CLAW'S LIGHTNING

    The Tesla Claw in the left hand (Akimbo) hit but showed no lightning: the left gun's lightning was asked for a
    moment after the picture was taken, and wiped before the next. It now shows in either hand.

THE LEFT VISIBOMB

    Fired from the left hand, the Visibomb's view went flat green, with no picture on the gun's screen. It now
    flies as from the right hand: the missile's view on the gun, the stick steering it.

PC VR: THE HUD AND MENUS FLICKERING

    A PC VR player (SteamVR, a Quest through Steam Link) saw the HUD and menus flicker, more in some starts than
    others, gone after closing and reopening the game. We could not make it happen here. Other OpenGL games have
    found SteamVR changing their drawing state in some of its calls, which fits a flicker a restart clears, so
    the game now puts back anything those calls change. If it still flickers, please send your log: it now says
    which call changed what.

ALSO NEW: 0.1.23, THE RELEASE JUST BEFORE

    The wrench flies the way you throw it, with aim help to start (Wrench throw aim, Moves). In a party: other
    players' fingers and height, a bolt you turn opens things for everyone with a host on 0.1.23 or later,
    RAC-Board with Multiplayer, and a host no longer taking itself for a member after a pause.

0.1.22: THE REFRESH RATE STEPS DOWN

    With Keep the refresh rate off (Graphics), a rate the game cannot hold steps down to 90 Hz before 72.

0.1.21: THIRD PERSON BUTTONS, THE TRESPASSER'S RINGS, THE PDA'S VENDOR

    Third person buttons (VR tab): each third person action on the button you choose, or Like first person. The
    Trespasser's rings one notch a push, or turned by hand (Grab the Trespasser's rings). A crash at the PDA's
    vendor fixed. Every scene the game draws itself checked to play in VR.

0.1.20: THE LAUNCHER'S VIEW, SMOOTHER FRAMES AT 120 HZ, MAGNEBOOTS

    The game always starts in the launcher's View. Smoother frames runs the headset at 120 Hz so the game draws 60
    pictures a second. Notices fade away. Your eyes at Ratchet's height on the Magneboots' walls and ceilings. A
    crash after drowning with something in the left hand fixed; glows in the take-off face your eyes.

0.1.19: THIRD PERSON AS A GAMEPAD, THE SEAS, WEAPONS ON THE BOARD, MULTIPLAYER IN VR

    Third person as one Xbox controller with a D-pad (VR tab), and Xbox-style buttons in the hints. The seas drawn
    wherever you look, Max draw distance reaching everything, and the city and the plants whole again. RAC-Board
    steering to where you look, with weapons on the board. Getting out of the ship, Magneboots staying on magnet
    paths, the weapon wheel's own page and back to an empty hand, punches with the Swingshot and its grab's throws,
    Foveated rendering. Multiplayer on a Quest or a PC through OpenRAC's server (everyone on 0.1.19 or later with
    matching mods: Multiplayer 0.12.2), VR players shown with their heads and hands, the wheel's slow motion and the
    Swingshot's grab in parties, and Update buttons for the built-in mods.

0.1.18: GRAB ENEMIES, GRAB MOVES AND PUNCHES, HANDS ON THE GUNS, MODS

    Swingshot grabs enemies, Grab moves and Punch with your hands (Moves, each off to start). The wrench thrown
    where your controller points; the weapon wheel at each hand, keeping your Swingshot; Ratchet's hands on the
    guns' grips and steady two-handed guns; gloves and boots fitted to his body; the wrench meeting the world; the
    ship's scenes in first person; steadier frames with AppSW. RAC-Board and Multiplayer built in (off to start),
    Browse OpenRAC's mods and Mod files, PC shortcuts for Playnite or Steam, and testers' crashes fixed.

0.1.17: SMOOTHER FRAMES, SLOW MOTION AT THE WHEEL, SEATED PLAY

    Smoother frames (AppSW, Graphics, Quest). Time slows while the weapon wheel is open, and you point at the
    weapon you want (More controls: Wheel slow motion, Wheel pick). Seated no longer lifts your view and hands;
    diving with both hands up, a look down and a stroke, or the left trigger; the wrench held through an eased
    grip; the hoverboard looking ahead (Moves, Hoverboard view). World scale in VR Body, Feet on the floor, the
    Help Desk on for a new game, and a long press of the left menu button to switch first and third person. Fixed:
    the PC VR HUD's text and the Trespasser's rings, the bolt numbers, water up close, PC VR movie pacing, the
    wheel's ammo counts and the title screen.

0.1.16: LONG MENUS NOW TELL YOU THERE IS MORE

    They are in the game, not in this launcher. In a level, press Y, then Options, VR Options, VR Controls, More
    controls, Moves. A page shows eight rows at a time, and the box under the list now says where the rest are:
    More below, More up/down or More above. Hold your hand on the bottom row and the list moves on, on the top
    row and it moves back. The Controls tab has a Menus section with where every option is.

0.1.15: FIXED THAT DARN THUMB!

    Sorry about that one. 0.1.14 turned your thumb the wrong way, back toward your wrist, and it got out because
    we did not test it thoroughly enough before the release. Our apologies for the hiccup.

    It now moves like a thumb. Lifted off the controller, it rests open beside your hand. On a button, the stick
    or the thumb rest, it comes in along your fingers. Close your hand and a lifted thumb is a thumbs-up, and a
    thumb on the controller folds over your fingers. Both hands, on Quest and on PC.

    We looked at it on a PC from all around each hand, with a simulated headset. Tell us how it looks in yours.

What 0.1.14 brought, in case you missed it:

1.  Now on PC VR too. The release has a Windows download beside this APK: the same launcher, options and game,
    as RatchetClankVR.exe, for SteamVR, Meta Horizon Link, Virtual Desktop, Pico Connect, Windows Mixed
    Reality and other PC VR software. Touch, Index, Vive, Pico, WMR and Cosmos controllers are known by name;
    others (PSVR2, Steam Frame, DPVR) come through SteamVR.

2.  Wall run and jump (new, in play: VR Options, VR Controls, More controls, Moves; Off to start). In the air by a
    wall, the stick along it runs Ratchet along the wall, and the stick into it runs him up it. A zip that ends
    at a wall holds him there instead of dropping him. The jump button leaps him off where your head looks,
    even with the stick still held along the wall, and his speed goes with him, so zips, swings and walls chain.

3.  Swingshot reach (new, Moves): Normal is the game's, Longer is twice that, No limit reaches any surface you can
    aim at, right across a level. A far zip goes faster than a near one. A left hand's shot too far to swing
    from zips instead. With Swingshot anywhere off, it is how far the game's own targets can be hooked from.

4.  The wrench thrown far (new, Moves). Wrench throw distance: Far, Very far and Super far go 2, 4 and 8 times as
    far, in an arc. Wrench comes back: When you call it, it stays where it lands until you squeeze the right
    grip or pull the trigger, and it comes whirling back with your hand buzzing harder as it nears. Every
    catch now has a thump in the hand, whichever you choose.

5.  Swingshot anywhere has moved to Moves, with a tester's notes in it. Fling: yank the hand that fired, hard, away
    from where you want to go, on a swing to throw it on, on a zip to leap off it. Zips chain in the air, with
    no need to touch the ground between them. The hook no longer cancels the Heli-Pack's glide: he stays up
    while it flies, and with jump held the glide comes back. A press aimed at nothing keeps trying while you
    hold it.

6.  The Swingshot in your hand. With one in each hand, each has its own hook: the hook no longer lags behind or
    hangs in the air, and firing one no longer fires the other. It now lies along your aim, however you hold the
    controller. If you set a Swingshot angle before (VR Hands), put it back to Default.

7.  Keep the refresh rate (new, Graphics, On to start). The headset stays at the rate you chose, such as 120 Hz,
    so wherever the game keeps up it is that smooth. Before, where the game could not hold the rate for twenty
    seconds, the headset dropped back to 72 Hz for the rest of the run. Off brings that back.

8.  The left grip only closes your hand, on foot in first person, Physical crouch on or off. The left trigger
    crouches and works the packs. With Akimbo's gun in the left hand, or in third person, the grip still
    crouches.

9.  Empty hand angle starts at 65 down. If yours was at the old start, 0, it moves to 65 down once; an angle
    you set yourself stays.

10. Walking backwards, Ratchet no longer slides forward when you let go of the stick. Walking aside, he no
    longer slides ahead either.

11. No warped picture at the start: the copyright lines after the intro movie, and the save select's picture
    as a level's first scene begins, are no longer held in front of you as you move your head.
