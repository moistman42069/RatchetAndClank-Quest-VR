NEW IN 0.1.17

SLOW MOTION AT THE WEAPON WHEEL (new, on to start)

    Hold the weapon wheel open and time slows to a third, so you can change weapons in the middle of a fight or a
    chain of moves. A sharp whoosh as it slows, the sound drops in pitch, a click at each weapon you pass, a quick
    chirp as the new weapon comes out, and normal speed snaps back with a thump. A quick press to talk to someone
    slows nothing. To change it: More controls, Wheel slow motion: With sounds, No sounds or Off.

POINT AT THE WEAPON YOU WANT (new, on to start)

    No more moving your whole arm toward a weapon. Open the wheel and point your hand at the weapon, as you point at
    a menu: a beam and a dot show where, and the weapon lights up. Let go and it comes out. Both hands, in first
    person, on foot. In a seat the reach still picks. To change it: More controls, Wheel pick: Point, or Reach for the
    old way.

SMOOTHER FRAMES (new, on to start, on Quest)

    The game draws every other frame, and the Quest's own Application SpaceWarp (AppSW) makes the ones in between
    from how things move. That gives the headset twice the time for each picture, so the view should stay smooth at
    90 or 120 Hz where it stuttered. Fast, thin things can smear a little at their edges, most of all when you turn
    with the stick. To turn it off: Graphics, Smoother frames (AppSW). On PC your headset's own smoothing does this
    instead. We could only try it in a simulated headset: tell us how it looks on your Quest.

FROM A SEATED PLAYER'S REPORT

    Seated no longer lifts your view and hands. Up to 0.1.16, VR Options, Play position: Seated put them 45 cm
    above Ratchet's eyes, though a recentre already puts your eyes at his, sitting or standing. Your hands were then
    out of the water at the surface, and above bolt cranks and enemies. Now Seated lifts nothing, choosing it
    recentres, and leaning forward in your chair does not crouch him. For a higher or lower view: VR Body, View
    height.

    Diving: at the surface, push both hands up quickly, however you hold the controllers, or look well down and
    stroke. The left trigger dives too, now in third person as well.

    The wrench stays in your hand through a swing. Easing the grip a little in a fast swing used to let go of it,
    or throw it. Now it holds until you open the grip well past halfway. Opening your hand in a swing still throws.

    The hoverboard looks where it goes, steady, not along Ratchet's side-on chest. To change it: More controls,
    Moves, Hoverboard view: Ahead, or Side-on for the old way.

    World scale is in the game too: VR Body, World scale, 70 to 150 percent.

FIXED

1.  PC VR: the HUD's text and numbers, and the Trespasser's rings. On SteamVR they did not show at all, which made the
    Trespasser impossible to do. They are there now. On Quest the same text was pale, and it is solid now.

2.  The bolt numbers at a vendor or an equipment box, on Quest and PC: no more lighter boxes behind the digits.

3.  Water from up close, on Quest and PC: parts of it were cut away when you stood right by it. It is whole now.

4.  PC VR: movies. They ran at a flat 60 pictures a second whatever your headset's rate, which could look laggy. They
    now keep to the headset's rate, with the sound still setting the movie's time. Tell us if yours still lags.

5.  The ammo count in the wheel: on its later pages, the count under a weapon's name was missing or was another
    weapon's. It is now that weapon's own.

6.  The title screen: Ratchet, his ship and the crate vanished as soon as the menu came up, and flashed back when you
    picked something. They now stay in the scene behind the menu.

ALSO NEW

7.  Feet on the floor (VR Body, off to start). You stand at Ratchet's height, so your real feet are below his ground.
    On, the view lifts so that his ground is your floor. It measures you each time you recentre, so stand up straight
    when you do. Your hands then reach from your own height, so some things are further from them. Off is as before.

8.  The game's Help Desk is on for a new game. It is the game's tips, in voice and text, like the one that explains
    the Trespasser when you find it. Up to 0.1.16 this port turned it off on every new game, so those tips never
    played. A save you already have keeps what it had: to turn the tips on or off there, in a level press Y, then
    Options, HelpDesk, Voice and Text.

9.  Switch the view quickly. In play, hold the left menu button for a second to switch between first and third
    person. A short press still recentres, as before. VR Options, View does the same.

10. Dive with the left trigger, in first or third person. At the water's surface, pull the left trigger and Ratchet
    dives. Under the water he goes where you look: look down and push the stick, or stroke, to go deeper, and look
    up to come back up. Pushing the water up with both hands still dives too.

GOOD TO KNOW: ALL OF THIS WORKS IN FIRST PERSON

    Bolt cranks: hold the wrench's head on the bolt (right grip), then walk around it or push the left stick
    forward. The hoverboard: the left stick steers and the left trigger boosts. The whole list is in the Controls
    tab.

0.1.16: LONG MENUS NOW TELL YOU THERE IS MORE

    They are in the game, not in this launcher. In a level, press Y, then Options, VR Options, VR Controls, More
    controls, Moves. A page shows eight rows at a time, and the box under the list now says where the rest are:
    More below, More up/down or More above. Hold your hand on the bottom row and the list moves on, on the top
    row and it moves back. The Controls tab has a Menus section with where every option is.

0.1.15: FIXED THAT DARN THUMB!

    Sorry about that one. 0.1.14 turned your thumb the wrong way, back toward your wrist, and it got out because
    we did not test it thoroughly enough before the release. Our apologies for the hiccup.

    It now moves like a thumb. Lifted off the controller, it rests open beside your hand. On a button, the stick
    or the thumb rest, it comes in along your fingers. Close your hand and a lifted thumb is a thumbs-up, and a
    thumb on the controller folds over your fingers. Both hands, on Quest and on PC.

    We looked at it on a PC from all around each hand, with a simulated headset. Tell us how it looks in yours.

What 0.1.14 brought, in case you missed it:

1.  Now on PC VR too. The release has a Windows download beside this APK: the same launcher, options and game,
    as RatchetClankVR.exe, for SteamVR, Meta Horizon Link, Virtual Desktop, Pico Connect, Windows Mixed
    Reality and other PC VR software. Touch, Index, Vive, Pico, WMR and Cosmos controllers are known by name;
    others (PSVR2, Steam Frame, DPVR) come through SteamVR.

2.  Wall run and jump (new, in play: VR Options, VR Controls, More controls, Moves; Off to start). In the air by a
    wall, the stick along it runs Ratchet along the wall, and the stick into it runs him up it. A zip that ends
    at a wall holds him there instead of dropping him. The jump button leaps him off where your head looks,
    even with the stick still held along the wall, and his speed goes with him, so zips, swings and walls chain.

3.  Swingshot reach (new, Moves): Normal is the game's, Longer is twice that, No limit reaches any surface you can
    aim at, right across a level. A far zip goes faster than a near one. A left hand's shot too far to swing
    from zips instead. With Swingshot anywhere off, it is how far the game's own targets can be hooked from.

4.  The wrench thrown far (new, Moves). Wrench throw distance: Far, Very far and Super far go 2, 4 and 8 times as
    far, in an arc. Wrench comes back: When you call it, it stays where it lands until you squeeze the right
    grip or pull the trigger, and it comes whirling back with your hand buzzing harder as it nears. Every
    catch now has a thump in the hand, whichever you choose.

5.  Swingshot anywhere has moved to Moves, with a tester's notes in it. Fling: yank the hand that fired, hard, away
    from where you want to go, on a swing to throw it on, on a zip to leap off it. Zips chain in the air, with
    no need to touch the ground between them. The hook no longer cancels the Heli-Pack's glide: he stays up
    while it flies, and with jump held the glide comes back. A press aimed at nothing keeps trying while you
    hold it.

6.  The Swingshot in your hand. With one in each hand, each has its own hook: the hook no longer lags behind or
    hangs in the air, and firing one no longer fires the other. It now lies along your aim, however you hold the
    controller. If you set a Swingshot angle before (VR Hands), put it back to Default.

7.  Keep the refresh rate (new, Graphics, On to start). The headset stays at the rate you chose, such as 120 Hz,
    so wherever the game keeps up it is that smooth. Before, where the game could not hold the rate for twenty
    seconds, the headset dropped back to 72 Hz for the rest of the run. Off brings that back.

8.  The left grip only closes your hand, on foot in first person, Physical crouch on or off. The left trigger
    crouches and works the packs. With Akimbo's gun in the left hand, or in third person, the grip still
    crouches.

9.  Empty hand angle starts at 65 down. If yours was at the old start, 0, it moves to 65 down once; an angle
    you set yourself stays.

10. Walking backwards, Ratchet no longer slides forward when you let go of the stick. Walking aside, he no
    longer slides ahead either.

11. No warped picture at the start: the copyright lines after the intro movie, and the save select's picture
    as a level's first scene begins, are no longer held in front of you as you move your head.
