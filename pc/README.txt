RATCHET & CLANK VR FOR PC
An unofficial fan port: Ratchet & Clank (2002, PlayStation 2) in PC VR, on OpenRAC's native port.
The same game, options and launcher as the Meta Quest APK, for a headset on a Windows PC.

START
  1. Keep this whole folder together, anywhere you can write (Desktop, Documents, a games drive).
  2. Start your headset's PC VR software: SteamVR, Meta Horizon Link (Link or Air Link),
     Virtual Desktop, Pico Connect, Windows Mixed Reality or another OpenXR one.
  3. Open RatchetClankVR.exe. The first time it asks for your own disc image (.iso) of the game
     and sets the game up from it, once. Then press PLAY IN VR.

NEEDS
  Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit; a PC and graphics card that run PC VR; a PC VR headset.
  Your own copy of the game as an .iso: PAL SCES-50916 or NTSC-U SCUS-97199 (the launcher says
  which exactly). No game files come with this release.

WHERE THINGS ARE
  Your set-up, saves and choices: %LOCALAPPDATA%\RatchetQuestVR
  Logs of every run and set-up:  Downloads\RatchetQuestVR (send the newest with any report)
  An update keeps all of them; the launcher's Updates card installs one for you.
